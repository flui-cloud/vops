Always-on service

Keeps the dashboard reachable and checks your hosts in the background — that is what lets vops notice a server going down while nothing is open.

Credential vault

Your provider tokens, sealed with a passphrase. The key is derived in memory and never written to disk; it stays open for 12 hours of use.
